About Kasi Cafe:

Kasi Cafe was founded in 2019, born from a love for great coffee and a great community

What started off as a small pop-up stall at the local market has grown into a loved neighbourhood cafe.

Our mission is simple:Serve exceptional food and drinks in an environment where everyone feels at home.We support local farmers,bakers and we are proud to be a part of the kasi community
